📺 Upload to YouTube

1 Export from Blockbench

In Blockbench, go to File → Export → Export Animation as Video or use a screen recorder to capture the animation preview.

2 Prepare Your Video File

Recommended format: MP4 (H.264 codec), 1080p resolution, 30 or 60 FPS. Keep file size under 128 MB for quick uploads.

3 Go to YouTube Studio

Visit studio.youtube.com and click Create → Upload Video.

4 Fill in Video Details

Add title, description, tags (e.g., "Minecraft", "Blockbench", "Animation"), and choose a catchy thumbnail.

5 Set Visibility & Publish

Choose Public, Unlisted, or Private. Click Publish when ready!

💡 Pro Tip: Add voice-over using CapCut before uploading for more engaging content!

🎙️ Add Voice-Over in CapCut

1 Download CapCut

Get CapCut from capcut.com (available for desktop and mobile). It's free!

2 Import Your Animation

Open CapCut, create a new project, and import your exported Minecraft animation video.

3 Record Voice-Over

Click Audio → Record and narrate your animation. Speak clearly and match your timing to the character movements.

4 Edit Audio

Adjust volume levels, add background music from CapCut's library, and use noise reduction if needed.

5 Add Effects & Text

Enhance with transitions, captions, stickers, or effects to make your video pop!

6 Export & Share

Export in 1080p, then upload directly to YouTube or save for later.

🎯 Voice Tips: Write a script first, record in a quiet room, and do multiple takes to get the best version!
